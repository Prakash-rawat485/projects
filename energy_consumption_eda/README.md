# 🌍 Project Overview

Electricity demand changes continuously throughout the day, week, month, and year. Understanding these consumption patterns helps power companies improve grid reliability, forecast demand, optimize energy distribution, and reduce operational costs.

This project performs a **complete Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)** on the **AEP Hourly Energy Consumption Dataset**, identifying seasonal trends, daily demand behavior, yearly changes, and time-based consumption patterns using Python.

---

# ✨ Objectives

✔ Understand electricity consumption behavior

✔ Detect seasonal demand changes

✔ Analyze hourly usage patterns

✔ Compare weekday and weekend consumption

✔ Study yearly demand variations

✔ Generate meaningful business insights

# 📈 Univariate Analysis

### ⚡ Energy Consumption Distribution

### Findings

- Most electricity demand remains within a stable range.
- Few extreme peaks indicate unusually high consumption.
- Outliers likely correspond to severe weather conditions or special events.

---

### 📅 Monthly Distribution

### Findings

- All months contain nearly equal observations.
- Dataset is balanced.
- No sampling bias observed.

---

# 📊 Bivariate Analysis

## ⏰ Hourly Energy Consumption

### Key Insights

🌙 Lowest demand occurs between **3 AM–5 AM**

🌅 Rapid increase begins around **6 AM**

🏢 Peak demand continues during business hours

🌃 Consumption decreases after evening

---

## 📅 Weekday vs Weekend

### Findings

- Weekdays consume more electricity.
- Weekend demand is comparatively lower.
- Commercial activities significantly affect power consumption.

---

## ❄️🌞 Monthly Energy Consumption

### Insights

- Winter demand is high.
- Summer demand is also high.
- Spring and Autumn show relatively lower usage.
- Weather strongly influences electricity consumption.

---

## 📆 Yearly Trend

### Observations

- Highest demand observed around **2007–2008**
- Noticeable decline after **2008–2009**
- Gradual reduction in later years

Possible reasons include:

- Improved energy efficiency
- Economic slowdown
- Better appliances
- Demand-side management

---

## 📊 2005 vs 2017 Comparison

### Insights

- Similar seasonal behavior observed.
- Winter and summer remain high-demand periods.
- Overall demand slightly changes across years.
- Weather remains the strongest influencing factor.

---

# 📌 Key Insights

✅ Electricity follows clear daily cycles.

✅ Early morning records the lowest demand.

✅ Weekdays consume significantly more energy.

✅ Weather strongly impacts electricity demand.

✅ Winter and Summer represent peak consumption periods.

✅ Electricity demand evolves gradually over years.
